Classic Pomodoro Default

The timeless 25/5 rhythm. 4 focused sprints followed by a 15-minute restorative long break.

Super-Pomodoro 3 Stages

Adapts to cognitive peaks: Phase 1 (Ignition 20m) → Phase 2 (Deep Flow Stopwatch with 5:1 Flowtime Rest) → Phase 3 (Animedoro 50m Reward).

52 / 17 Method DeskTime

The data-driven sprint rule: 52 minutes of intense distraction-free focus paired with 17 minutes of complete disconnection.

Ultradian Rhythm 90 / 20

Mirrors natural 90-minute human biological energy cycles. 90 minutes deep work & 20 minutes biological recovery.

Animedoro Reward Block

50-minute deep push rewarded with 20 minutes of guilt-free media (one anime episode or TV show without ads).

DeskTime 112 / 26 Deep Sprint

DeskTime's post-remote work finding for extreme immersion: 112-minute deep flow sessions with 26-minute recovery walks.
